01 / Context

Single-up bonding was the bottleneck on a qualified program.

Operators spent most of the shift aligning parts by hand before each bond run. Each manual setup risked drifting outside qualified tolerances, and throughput was limited to one part at a time.

02 / System

Multi-up, spring-retained fixture with a machined datum edge.

Designed a machined aluminum alignment edge referenced to the platform datum so products locate without operator judgment. Spring retention holds each part down consistently across the carrier. Drove all critical dimensions from the bond head tolerance budget rather than the housing envelope.
